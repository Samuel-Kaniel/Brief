# Brag Plan: Brief

## What is this app?
Brief is an Expo news reader that pulls live public RSS into a dark swipeable card deck — pick topics, swipe right to save or left to skip, and get one on-device daily digest.

## The angle
The feed is the product. This is not a generic “stay informed” SaaS reel. The video should feel like opening Brief: charcoal cards, blue topic chips, a real headline, a save/skip swipe, then the quiet promise of one digest at 8 AM.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
White type on Brief’s `#0f1115` void: **The news, without the scroll.** Fast-in, then hold. No logo yet.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Recreated Brief feed card (HEALTH / NPR GLP-1 story, from the real screenshot photo) inside a phone frame, wordmark **Brief** above.
- Onboarding chips lighting up one by one: Technology, AI / ML, Science, Finance — then **Start reading**.
- A right-swipe SAVE bookmark, then a left-swipe SKIP with the real **Skipped · Undo** bar.

## Outro / punchline
**Brief.** then **Read it in a minute.** Hold. No URL, no store badge.

## User flow worth showing
Pick topics → swipe the deck (save / skip) → one daily digest from those topics.

## Tone
- Preset: `app-store`
- Creative direction: polished iOS feature-card film of a real RSS reader
- Interpretation: clean title-case feature lines, 4–6 scenes, slide/wipe transitions, restraint on SFX; the app UI does the talking.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0f1115`
- Accent: `#2563eb` (selected chips, Start reading, digest switch)
- Accent light: `#93c5fd` (header icons, Back, Undo)
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Secondary text: `#9ca3af` / `#9aa1ad`
- Card: `#12151c`
- Chip border: `#374151`
- Display font: Inter (SF-like stand-in for RN system type)
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: full-bleed news card with category pill + headline + source footer

## Share copy (draft)
Brief is a swipeable RSS reader: pick topics, save or skip, and get one daily digest — no API key.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (steady, clean; best for polished / app-store)
- Music treatment: start at 0s, volume ~0.32, fade under the final logo from ~18.4s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12` at ~110 BPM. Strong-cue targets: 8.74s (Start reading), 10.93s (save swipe), 17.47s (outro name). Chip arrivals on beat grid 6.00 / 6.56 / 7.09 / 7.64.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass/RMS breathes the phone-frame presence and a soft blue glow — no waveform bars
- SFX posture: sparse / moderate; motion-matched; professional restraint
- Audio-coupled moments: chip selects, Start reading tap, card swipe/place, undo bar, logo land
- Restraint rule: no voiceover; no dense casino stack; 4–6 SFX max
